# Low-performing classes, slots and instructors: the quarterly review

**Decision (Kenny, 11 Oct 2026):** whenever the Coordinator spots a low-performing class, timeslot or instructor, it follows
this process. It was chosen over two alternatives: a fast one-off consolidation, and a 12-week rescue run slot by slot.
Every slot sits in the same 13-week cycle under published rules, so no one is singled out and the timetable corrects itself
each quarter.

The agent scores, drafts, tracks and writes the messages. People decide: a coordinator signs each decision and Kenny
approves every removal (design principle 3 in `03-proposal.md`).

## 1. The cycle

Cycles are calendar quarters (1 Jan, 1 Apr, 1 Jul, 1 Oct), so each rate and free-access review in those months has a full
quarter's bands behind it. The first cycle is short: 1 Nov to 31 Dec 2026 (9 weeks). The week numbers below are for a 13-week
quarter; in the short cycle the first two steps keep their weeks and the last three count back from the end (the review pack
in the last 3 weeks, the notices in the last 2).

| Week | Step | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Score every slot and assign its band (section 3). Publish the bands to the coordinators, and to each instructor for their own slots. | Agent drafts, coordinator checks |
| 2 | Red slots marked "Under review" on the timetable and announced in class. Regulars of each Red slot identified. One intervention chosen per Red and Amber slot (SOP-2). | Coordinator with the instructor |
| 3 to 10 | Interventions run. Weekly headcount of Red and Amber slots in the Monday KPI report. | Agent tracks |
| 11 (the last 3 weeks) | Review pack: each Red slot drafted as keep, move or remove, with its evidence. Coordinator signs, Kenny approves removals. Instructors affected told in writing the same week. | Agent drafts, people decide |
| 12 (the last 2 weeks) | Next quarter's timetable published. Final notice to members of removed or moved slots. Personal messages to regulars with no other GX class. | Coordinator, club manager |
| 13 (the last week) | Last classes of removed slots. | |
| Next week 1 | New timetable live. New slots enter the incubator. | |

Members of a Red slot get about 11 weeks of warning in total: "Under review" from week 2, then the final notice in week 12.

## 2. What is measured

- **Attendance** is corrected attendance: marked Attended, or booked, not cancelled and checked in at the host club within
  2 hours of the start. Marked attendance alone under-counts by about 13%.
- **Slot** = club + weekday + start time + class name (whitespace collapsed, upper-cased). Renamed classes are merged before
  scoring, so one class's history is not split in two.
- **Score** = average corrected headcount over the trailing 12 weeks. Bands use headcount, not fill %, until studio
  capacities in Sentinel are reset to real room sizes.
- **Minimums:** a slot is banded only with 6 or more occurrences in the window, and is eligible for removal only with 8 or
  more. No instructor is assessed on fewer than 8 classes.

## 3. Bands and what each one triggers

| Band | Rule | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Green | 10 or more | Protected. Waitlist on. Full at 90%+ of capacity in 60%+ of occurrences: a candidate for a second session. |
| Amber | 6 to 9.9 | One instructor-led intervention per quarter (SOP-2). Amber two quarters running: treated as Red. |
| Red | Under 6 | "Under review" from week 2, one matched intervention for at least 6 weeks, regulars identified. Removed at quarter end if under 6 in 8 of the last 12 weeks, unless the weekly headcount rose 4 weeks running (then held one more quarter). |
| Incubator | First 8 weeks of a new slot or format | Not banded. Week 4 check: under 4 average means fix the time, name or promotion now. Enters the bands in week 9. |

**Early trigger:** two nil-booking weeks in a quarter put a slot straight into review, whatever its band.

**Licensed formats (Les Mills):** once a licence is paid, an extra class costs only the instructor, and cutting one saves only
that while raising the licence cost per attendance of every class left. So a licensed slot is moved or swapped before it is
cut. Separately, in week 11 each licence's cost per corrected attendance is checked against a ceiling of RM 10. A licence over
the ceiling for two months running is either consolidated into fewer, fuller slots or replaced by a non-licensed format, and
the 3-month Les Mills notice period applies.

## 4. Find the cause before choosing the remedy

Each low slot gets one cause, assigned in writing in week 2.

| Cause | Test | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Time slot | Other classes at the same club and time band are low too (weekend afternoons, for example). | Move toward the club's busy band, or remove. No instructor or format fix will help. |
| Format | The format is low across slots and clubs. | Replace with a non-licensed format the club already fills; do not add slots. |
| Cannibalisation | The same format at the same club has strong and weak slots. | Merge the weak slot into the strong one; give the instructor a second session of a full class. |
| Instructor | Same format, club and time band, different instructor, much fuller. | SOP-3. Only this cause leads to an instructor conversation. |
| Data | Marked and corrected attendance disagree widely, or the class was renamed. | Fix the data. The slot is not judged this cycle. |

Slot and format explain most low attendance. Pay rate has almost no relationship with turnout (correlation 0.1 across 41
instructors in the Jun-Sep 2026 data), so instructors are compared only within the same format and time band.

## 5. Before any slot is removed

All seven are done and logged, or the slot stays.

1. **Data check.** Corrected attendance used, renames merged, 8+ occurrences.
2. **Cause assigned in writing** and signed by the coordinator. If the coordinator teaches the slot, Kenny signs instead.
3. **One matched intervention** run for at least 6 weeks, with weekly headcounts.
4. **Regulars contacted.** Members with 4+ attendances in the window are each told personally what is changing and which class to try instead. Those with no other GX attendance go to the club manager for a call within 2 weeks of the change.
5. **Instructor told first, in writing, in week 11**, at least a week before members and 3 weeks before the change, with a redeployment offer (cover list, a second session of a full class, an incubator slot) made in the same conversation. An instructor never hears it from a member.
6. **Members told in week 12**, at least 2 weeks before the last class: in class by the instructor, by app push to everyone who booked in the last 12 weeks, and on the timetable ("Final class <date>", with the replacement). The reason is given plainly, for example "fewer than 6 people on average since June".
7. **The replacement is published with the removal.** A blank hour on the timetable reads as decline.

**After removal:** the instructor is paid in full for every class up to the last. The regulars are tracked for 8 weeks. If
more than a third of them stop attending GX altogether, the removal is reviewed and the slot may be reinstated.

**Safeguard:** no instructor loses all their classes in one cycle.

## 6. SOPs

**SOP-1: Scoring.** Monthly on the 1st working day (full scoring) and weekly in the Monday KPI report (headcount of Red and
Amber slots). The agent applies corrected attendance and scores every slot with 6+ occurrences: 12-week average, 4-week trend,
regulars count, regulars with no other GX class, and licence cost per attendance for licensed formats. Red list on top.

**SOP-2: Intervention menu** (coordinator with the instructor, in week 2). Pick one, matched to the cause, and run it for at
least 6 weeks:
- Time slot: move within 30 minutes toward the club's busy band, or swap days with a strong slot as a test.
- Format: rename the class, shorten it to 45 minutes, or replace it with a non-licensed format the club already fills.
- Cannibalisation: merge into the strong slot and move the instructor to a second session of a full class.
- Instructor: SOP-3.
- Any cause: the instructor brings 3 guests over 3 weeks; the class is promoted on club social channels and at the front desk for 4 weeks; the coordinator attends once and gives written feedback.

**SOP-3: Instructor conversation** (only when section 4 isolates the instructor). In private, within a week. Show the
comparison (same format, club and band) and ask what they think is happening before saying anything else. Agree two specific
behaviours, for example: arrive 10 minutes early and greet members by name, and end each class by inviting members to the
next one. The coordinator or a senior instructor attends twice in 6 weeks and gives written feedback on those two behaviours.
At 6 weeks the options are to carry on, to redeploy to a format or time that suits the instructor better, or to team-teach
with the instructor whose version of the class fills.

**SOP-4: Member communication for a removal.** As section 5, steps 4 to 7. The agent drafts every message; nothing is sent
without the coordinator's approval. Week +2: the club manager calls flagged regulars who have not booked another class. Week
+8: the agent reports how many regulars moved to another class.

**SOP-5: New slots.** Opened only where a full class shows demand, never speculatively. Launch with a promotion plan, 2 guest
passes per regular of the nearest full class, and the waitlist on from day one. Incubator rules in section 3.

## 7. Data it depends on

- GX Class Detail (one row per booking) and Studio (one row per class) every week and every month, plus GX Class
  Summary and the Cancelled Event List monthly, from the scheduled Sentinel exports.
- Member-level check-ins, used for the attendance correction before they are reduced to counts: the result is a ledger
  per month (one row per booking outcome, membership number, no name) that the scoring reads.
- The review sheet: a shared Google Sheet with one row per slot and quarter (cause, intervention, decision, dates, who
  signed), exported read-only for each build. The dashboard shows the rules' suggestions beside the decisions.
- Regulars lists name members, so they live only on the private GX dashboard behind Cloudflare Access, never in this repo
  (section "Security and confidentiality" in `04-implementation-notes.md`).
